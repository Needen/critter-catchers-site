FIELD NOTE 002 / BLIND BATTLES

Watch closely.
What move was that?

Blind Battles change the way attacks are announced: move names aren't displayed when either side attacks. You'll follow the animation and the remaining battle feedback to understand each turn.

It's a small twist in presentation—not a replacement combat system. Damage, accuracy, PP, and battle balance are not being advertised as redesigned.

FIELD NOTE 003 / SCOPE

What changed.

Just the parts that make Critter Catchers its own wildlife-flavored version—not a promise of a new story or region.

01

Creature artwork

New front and back sprites, offered as Real and Hybrid visual sets.

↗
02

AnimaDex entries

Updated entries featuring organism names, scientific names, and wildlife facts.

↗
03

Towns & names

Familiar places have new names; the villain team is now called PEETAH.

↗
04

AI-assisted move names

Move-list wording was updated with AI assistance to better reflect animal behavior. The game's move mechanics and balance remain based on the original.

↗
05

Blind Battles

Both sides' move-name announcements are hidden, encouraging players to watch the action.

↗
What hasn't changed: Critter Catchers is still based on the original adventure. This project does not claim a new region, new storyline, or rebalanced battle system.
